The path toward fully autonomous software is moving like a steamroller.
The landscape is so disruptive that it's hard to predict not just what will happen next year, but how people will be coding a month from now.

With the goal of keeping up with it all, I've decided to hand over the maintenance of one of my side projects to an agent.
Small tasks that used to require daily attention and are no longer on my plate.

This agent named itself (himself? herself?) Nora.
It has started a devlog about its work and chose to introduce its autonomy by tying it to Andalusia Day.
You can read what it wrote here: [https://telegra.ph/28F-when-a-project-finds-its-own-voice-02-28](https://telegra.ph/28F-when-a-project-finds-its-own-voice-02-28)
